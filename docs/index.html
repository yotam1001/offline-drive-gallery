Stay local and read-only

Your files stay where they are. The app saves a catalog under your Windows profile without an account or server.

Three steps, no setup service

The catalog is a snapshot you can refresh whenever the drive changes.

Add a drive

Choose any drive or folder. The app reads file names and asks Windows for available media thumbnails.

Unplug it

Keep searching and paging through the saved gallery. Select a result to see its last known path.

Rescan later

Reconnect the drive and refresh the catalog. A canceled scan keeps the previous snapshot.

Built for a specific gap

Free Virtual Volumes View already catalogs disconnected disks well. WinCatalog offers thumbnails in a paid product. Offline Drive Gallery offers a small, open-source Windows gallery for this particular workflow.

It does not inspect media contents, OCR images, or keep a live backup. A rescan is needed after files change.

Your catalog stays on your PC

File names, former paths, and thumbnails are stored in a local SQLite database. The app makes no network requests. The database is not encrypted, so keep it private if your files are sensitive.

Thumbnail availability depends on the image and video handlers installed in Windows. Unsupported formats show a labeled placeholder.
